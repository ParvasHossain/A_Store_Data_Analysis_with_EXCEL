## 📊 Key Insights (2022)
* **Customer Demographics:** Women accounted for **64%** of total sales compared to **36%** by men, with **Adults** representing the largest buying segment[cite: 1].
* **Seasonal Trends:** Sales and order volume peaked in **March** (~1.90M+ revenue) and saw a secondary rise in **August** (~1.80M)[cite: 1].
* **Top Performing States:** **Maharashtra** (2.99M), **Karnataka** (2.65M), and **Uttar Pradesh** (2.10M) generated the highest revenue, followed by Telangana (1.71M) and Tamil Nadu (1.68M)[cite: 1].
* **Order Fulfillment:** **92%** of orders were successfully delivered, while cancellations (3%), returns (3%), and refunds (2%) accounted for the remaining 8%[cite: 1].
* **Sales Channels:** **Amazon**, **Flipkart**, and **Ajio** drove the majority of order volume[cite: 1].

## 💡 Business Recommendations
1. **Target Core Demographics:** Focus ad spend and promotional campaigns on **adult women** across Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh[cite: 1].
2. **Optimize Channel & Seasonal Campaigns:** Launch exclusive discounts on **Amazon** and **Flipkart** ahead of peak months (**March** and **August**) to maximize conversion[cite: 1].
